Research & projects

Accuracy Without Grounding

MSc Dissertation, University of Surrey — submitted 2026

Diagnosing and repairing a visual grounding failure in a contrastive-attention chest X-ray VQA pipeline. The model could answer questions accurately while attending to the wrong regions of the image entirely, a gap between correctness and grounding that has real consequences in clinical settings. This project traced the failure to its source and proposed a fix.
